# Product

**Name:** Turncoat — *Master the art of changing your mind.*

Turncoat is a debating and reasoning platform built around understanding,
defending, challenging and reversing perspectives. Flagship: a Turncoat
debate — argue one side, switch, defend the opposite, receive evaluation.

## Philosophy
Encourage intellectual flexibility, balanced and evidence-based reasoning,
articulation, current-affairs awareness, nuance and civil disagreement.
Never imply that changing position is weakness.

> A strong thinker should be able to understand a position deeply enough to
> defend it — even when they personally disagree with it.

## Audience
Primary: UPSC aspirants. Secondary: college/school students, competitive
debaters, general public. Modern, intelligent, ambitious — not an exam portal.

## Signature
THINK. DEFEND. SWITCH.
